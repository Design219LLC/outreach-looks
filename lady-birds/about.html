The room

Downtown Sheridan.

Lady Birds sits on West Center Street, across from the square. Black awning, stone and brick, bird on the glass.

It’s a cafe people come back to. Regulars know the door. The plate comes out hot and the portions run large.

Look at the folder or ask what’s on. Confirm hours at the door. Call if you need a table or a to-go order.

Photos on this site are from their public Facebook. We don’t use other restaurants’ pictures.
